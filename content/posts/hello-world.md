## Why a blog?

Writing is one of the best ways to solidify understanding. If I can explain something clearly in writing, I actually understand it. If I struggle to explain it, I don't — and that's valuable to know.

This blog is a place for me to:

- **Think out loud** about code and ideas
- **Document** things I keep forgetting and have to look up again
- **Share** tools and workflows that genuinely improved my life as a developer

## What to expect

Posts here will mostly be short and focused — a trick, a pattern, a tool, a lesson. I'd rather publish something small and useful than wait until I have something perfect.

No particular posting schedule. Just whenever I have something worth writing about.

## Stack

This blog runs on:

- [Hugo](https://gohugo.io/) — extremely fast static site generator
- [PaperMod](https://github.com/adityatelange/hugo-PaperMod) — clean, minimal theme
- [GitHub Pages](https://pages.github.com/) + [GitHub Actions](https://github.com/features/actions) — free hosting with automatic deploys on every push

If you're thinking of starting a blog, this is a solid, zero-cost setup. Maybe I'll write a post about it.
